Requirements

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 14 or later. Models download separately (from about 350 MB). 8 GB of memory runs models up to about 4B parameters; fine-tuning larger models needs 16 GB.

Getting Started

  • Open Models and download Qwen3-0.6B (free).
  • Go to Anatomy and press Run on Mac (⌘↩). Click through each stage.
  • Open Sampling Lab and move the sliders to see how the next token is chosen.

Common Questions

A model download is slow or failed. Models come from Hugging Face. Check your connection and choose Retry in Models.

A model says it needs more memory than this Mac has. Each model card compares its needs with your Mac's GPU memory budget. Pick a smaller model, or close other apps for models marked "tight".

Why can't I X-ray or fine-tune some models? X-ray needs standard attention layers to inspect; hybrid models (LFM2, Qwen3.5) and Gemma 4 use different layers. Qwen3.5's linear-attention layers can't be trained by MLX yet. Each model card explains why.

Restore purchases. Open the paywall and choose Restore, or use LLM Lens ▸ Restore Purchases.

Cancel a subscription. System Settings ▸ your name ▸ Media & Purchases ▸ Subscriptions, or App Store ▸ your account ▸ Subscriptions.

Where are my fine-tuned adapters? Fine-tune ▸ Export adapter… or Export fused model… copies them to a folder you choose. Both work with mlx-lm and mlx-swift-lm.
