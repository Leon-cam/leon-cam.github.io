Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Overview

LLM Lens is a macOS app that runs open language models entirely on your Mac with Apple's MLX framework and visualizes how they work. It is designed so that your data stays on your device.

Data Collection

LLM Lens does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data Stored on Your Mac

Prompts, generated text, datasets you import, fine-tuned adapters and models you export, and downloaded model weights are stored only in the app's sandboxed container on your Mac, or in folders you choose when exporting. Deleting a model from the Models screen, or deleting the app, removes them.

Network Use

The app connects to the internet only to download model weights you choose to download, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). These requests go directly from your Mac to Hugging Face and identify only the model being downloaded. Your prompts and data are never sent. Hugging Face's handling of download requests is described in its privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Children

LLM Lens is a developer and education tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated.
